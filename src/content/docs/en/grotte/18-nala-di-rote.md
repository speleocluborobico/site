---
title: Nala di Rote
---
**LoBg 1244**

To reach the cave, which is located at Valpiana di Bedulita (BG), take the SP 16 road that climbs the orographic right side of the Valle Imagna towards Costa Imagna.

About 1 km before the junction leading to Valcava, near a large right-hand bend, once you have left the cars, you climb through the woods keeping to the right of a small valley. In no more than five minutes' walk along faint path tracks you reach Nala di Rote.

 The entrance, which lies in a small doline in the woods, has been camouflaged with boulders, given how dangerous the cave is.

## Exploration history

Nala was explored in the 1970s by Rocco Zimbelli using pioneering techniques, at the request of the owner of the land where the cave opens, who was convinced that water reserves would be found. It was then forgotten until 1988, when cavers from the Nottole tracked down its entrance and descended it, checking for any possible continuations. At the bottom an aven was climbed for about 15 m, reaching an extremely narrow meander, which in turn was followed for about thirty metres until it became prohibitively small. The absence of a draught ruled out any significant continuation. The survey of the cave was completed in 1994 by cavers from the SCO.

## Morphology

The cave, which opens in the Norian Dolomia Principale, consists of a single vertical (-78mt). The entrance is a narrow horizontal crawl about 3 m long leading to a small ledge. The first 20 metres are descended along a debris slope, to be tackled with caution if there are people below. The next pitch is a splendid p.55 with flowstone-coated walls, almost perfectly vertical. At the bottom a small slope about 4 m long takes us to a tiny pool about 1.5 m wide and 1 deep with completely still water. Beautiful speleothems hang down from the aven that has been explored, and a myriad of tiny helictites cover the walls of the small terminal chamber.
