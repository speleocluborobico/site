The Speleo Club Orobico adopts the [Creative Commons](http://www.creativecommons.it/) system, a copyright licence which, unlike traditional copyright stating «All rights reserved», allows reproduction of the work provided that certain conditions are met, summarised by the abbreviations below.

BY - means that attribution of the source must always be given;

NC - NonCommercial means that the content may not be used for commercial purposes, for example to earn money through advertising or sales;

SA - ShareAlike If you use the published articles, or only part of them, you must do so under the same Creative Commns licence.
