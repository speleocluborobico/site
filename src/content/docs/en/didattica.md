## Introductory Caving Courses

Every year, starting in September, the SCO organises an introductory course to the world of caving, a course that combines theory and practical lessons and leads participants to take their first steps into the underground world safely. Over the month and a half of the course, each participant is guided along a path that takes them from their first steps on the walls of an indoor climbing gym to the most important cave, usually visited on the last trip of the course. At the end of the eleven lessons, divided between theory (history, equipment, nutrition, geology, biospeleology, first aid, survey) and practice (from the indoor gym to the most demanding cave, in a carefully planned progression), each participant will receive a certificate of attendance, a document marking their underground initiation. The first post-course event will be a trip for the whole group to an important site in the Italian geological landscape, where the former course participant will finally have the chance to "take on" the underground world as a true caver.  
   
Throughout the course, the SCO provides beginners with all the necessary equipment, from harness to helmet to lighting system; participants may use the club's equipment for up to six months after the end of the course, so that they can consider buying their own with proper awareness.  To complete enrolment in the course, prior membership of the CAI for the current year is required.

## Internal Technical Improvement Courses

During the year, the Speleo Club Orobico organises various meetings and refresher courses for its members covering most underground activities, from knots to surveys, first aid, the role of assistant instructor and much more. Check the calendar or contact the SCO secretary's office for further information.

## Guided Trips

The Speleo Club Orobico is available to organise cave trips, accompanying anyone who wishes to approach the underground world in complete safety, providing both equipment and the mandatory insurance cover. Every year groups of schoolchildren, scouts or simply curious people are accompanied by SCO volunteers into caves that are easy to move through yet at the same time deeply moving, letting them experience first-hand the sensations that only the underground world can give.

The underground environment is not hostile to those who know it, but those experiencing it for the first time must be guided in the best possible way: for this reason, trips can only be organised when a good number of members are available to act as guides.

For information, contact the SCO secretary's office.
