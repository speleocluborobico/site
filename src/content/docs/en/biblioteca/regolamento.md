---
title: Rules
---
1. The Library owned by the S.C.O. CAI Bergamo consists of a collection of documentary material, connected in various ways to caving culture, in the form of journals, publications, books, slides, video cassettes, maps, topographic surveys, cadastral records, digital media and any other types.
2. The Library is available to members who are up to date with their annual membership fee and to non-members who submit a written request for access to the material. For non-members, the material is for viewing only.
3. The Library service is open during the group's meeting hours and when at least one of the persons in charge, or a substitute designated by them, is present.
4. Maps, topographic surveys and the cave register may be freely consulted by members at the Library, but are excluded from loan. The group's archive, consisting mostly of material not yet published, cannot be consulted by non-members.
5. Some works of particular importance (such as easily damaged documents, degree theses or guides containing information on caves, routes, surveys) are excluded from loan and may be consulted at the Library; the staff will record each access.
6. Documents available for loan will be handed over after being recorded in the dedicated loan register.
7. Up to two documents at a time may be borrowed or consulted. Special cases will be assessed individually by the librarians, after consulting the SCO board.
8. Loans last a maximum of thirty days and, if there are no reservations, may be renewed upon presentation of the documents in question.
9. If documents are kept beyond the due date, the offender will be suspended from borrowing for a period equal to three times the delay.
10. The user is responsible for the proper care of the documents borrowed. In the event of damage or loss, they must personally purchase a new copy or pay the group the cost incurred for its replacement.
