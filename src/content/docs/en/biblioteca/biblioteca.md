---
title: Library
---

In the Speleo Club Orobico library at Palamonti you will find material covering every branch of the underground world: educational books, biospeleology, descriptions of Italian and international caves, as well as current and historical journals, both our own and those of other groups, describing exploration progress, activities and trip programmes.

The archive is completed by multimedia material such as slides, photos and films.

All volumes in the library are available on loan to SCO members, or for consultation by non-members.
