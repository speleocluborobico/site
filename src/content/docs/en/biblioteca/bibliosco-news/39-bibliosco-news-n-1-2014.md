Third and final issue dedicated to the material collected at the “Speleopolis Underground” Gathering held in Casola from 30/10 to 3/11/2013. 

This time I cover the multimedia material, which I list below:

DVD

Ø  **SPELEOTEMI – Antro del Corchia: un archivio climatico**

(enclosed with Talp - Journal of the Federazione Speleologica Toscana – no.   - 2009)

Produced in 2007 by Fabio Malfatti – running time: 23’

The video discusses the studies carried out on samples of speleothems taken from the Antro del Corchia to study climate change over time.

Ø  **CRO RI DI LO MA**

A 2012 film by Antonio Giacomin about the 44th Introductory Caving Course of the Commissione Grotte Eugenio Boegan of the Società Alpina delle Giulie of Trieste.

Through one participant in particular, the video tells of the impressions gained while taking part in the course. Our “friends” Linus and Antonella “Tonza” Tizianel can also be spotted in the film.
