PUBLICATIONS

**DIMENSIONE BUIO** – edited by Leonardo Busellato and Gruppo Grotte Schio – (1991)

History and activities of the Gruppo Grotte C.A.I. Sezione di Schio 1930-1990. It also includes numerous descriptions of the caves in the karst area north of the Lessini ridge Priabona-Carega.

**L’ABISSO – Ottant’anni di esplorazioni nella Spluga della Preta** – by Francesco Sauro (2007)

A fantastic account of the exploration saga of one of Italy’s most historic caves.

I recommend it to everyone.

**APUANE E DINTORNI – Guida incompleta alla scoperta del fenomeno carsico – a cura del Comitato Apuane 2007** (2007) - Another cave guide published on the occasion of the “Metamorfosi? 2007” gathering held at the foot of the magical Alpi Apuane. It contains the descriptions of the main caves of the Apuane.

**IL RESPIRO DELLE GROTTE – Piccole divagazioni sulla profondità** - by Natalino Russo (2013)

Using a caving adventure as a pretext, the author tries  to talk about exploration in a somewhat broader sense, in an attempt to arouse the curiosity not only of explorers and travellers, but also of other mountain-goers. (mountain [blog.it](http://blog.it))

**100 ANNI SOTTOTERRA - Il Circolo Speleologico Romano dal 1904 al 2004** - edited by the Circolo Speleologico Romano – (2015)

After years of waiting, we have finally received the book we had ordered long ago.

Here you can watch the presentation book trailer: <https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ovx_zEia8gk>

**GROTTE AL CONFINE EST – Speleologia in Friuli Venezia Giulia** - by Sandro Sedran  (2015)

The latest editorial effort by the well-known Venetian caving photographer, a dear friend of ours, which covers, with beautiful photos and detailed descriptions, the finest caves of Friuli.

**GROTTA LUK – In ricordo di un amico** – Edited by Paolo Forconi and Luigi Russo (2015)

The story, in pictures, of a new exploration in Monte Sortatte, Lazio.

The proceeds from the sale of the book will go to the family of Lucas Ruiz Pérez, a Mexican caver who recently passed away.

MULTIMEDIA

**RANA-PISATELA 40 KM** - DVD

The documentary on the Buso della Rana - Buso della Pisatela karst system is a film of about half an hour that illustrates all aspects of this important karst system near Vicenza, from the history of its exploration, through geology, climate and hydrology, right up to its present-day use.

Here is the trailer: <https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I8kULZIbFDw>
