On the weekend of 23-24 March, spent in Liguria for a course, I was able to collect from the Gruppo Speleologico Savonese the back issues of their journal, which we had not been receiving for a while; in addition, our friend Rinaldo gave us a book that I tell you about below.  JOURNALS  **STALATTITI E STALAGMITI** (Caving periodical of the Gruppo Speleologico Savonese) :  no. 22 – year 1996;  no. 28 – year 2002;  no. 29 – years 2003-2004;  no. 30 – years 2005-2006;  no. 31 – year 2007;  no. 32 – year 2008  Gilberto Calandri of the G.S. Imperiese, for his part, gave all course participants a copy of the latest issue of their bulletin:  **BOLLETTINO** of the Gruppo Speleologico Imperiese C.A.I. no. 62 – January-December 2010  Index: see attached pdf file  Finally, from the other Savona group, we also received the:  **BOLLETTINO**  of the Gruppo Grotte C.A.I. Savona no. 8 - 2011  which also contains a curious experiment with 3D photos and the glasses needed to view them in three dimensions.  Index: see attached pdf file  BOOKS  As I mentioned above, Rinaldo Massucco, on behalf of the G.S.S., gave us this publication of theirs:  **“GROTTE E CARSISMO DELL'ALTA VAL BORMIDA - La Bormida di Millesimo”**  from the series "Grotte e Carsismo in Liguria".  The journals and the book described above can be borrowed for consultation from the club library; please refer to the library regulations for loan procedures.  Happy reading, and see you next time.
