|  |  |  |  |  |  |  |  |  |  |  |  |  |  | The regular round-up of news from the club library is back.  This issue is entirely dedicated to Cave Rescue.  **MEDIA LIBRARY**  Thanks to our member Giovanni Merisio, the double DVD “Sicuri in Montagna” has arrived at the club;  its contents are divided as follows:  -         DVD 1 : films  ·         1. La Protezione Civile della Provincia di Bergamo (8')  ·         2. Convenzione Provincia-Soccorso Alpino (1')  ·         3. Storie di Soccorso (38')  ·         4. Attività S.A.S.L. (11')  ·         5. "Di più non possiamo chiedere" (16')  -         DVD 2: Various Sicuri in Montagna booklets    The evening on the Explorations of the Buco del Castello organised by the SCO last 8 June was also attended by Lelo Pavanello and Sergio Orsini of the Gruppo Spelologico Bolognese-Unione Speleologica Bolognese; besides their welcome presence, they kindly brought us a series of publications as a gift, including the following    JOURNALS    Ø  Offprint of **SOTTOTERRA** (Newsletter of the Gruppo Speleologico Bolognese – Speleo club Bologna) no. 13 – 1965 with the article “La sciagura di Roncobello” about the accident at the Buco del Castello    Ø  Offprint of **SOTTOTERRA** (Speleology journal of the Gruppo Speleologico Bolognese)   no. 66 – 1982 with the report “24 aprile ’66: Abisso del Castello” about the accident at the Buco del Castello  Ø  **SOTTOTERRA** (Speleology journal of the Gruppo Speleologico Bolognese – Unione Speleologica Bolognese)   no. 120 – 2005.  This is a Special Issue of the journal dedicated to the fortieth anniversary of the deaths of Carlo Pelagalli and Luigi Donini and of the founding of Cave Rescue (it contains reports and articles on the accident at the Buco del Castello)  Contents: see attached pdf file  and the following  BOOKS  Ø  **“LA NASCITA DEL SOCCORSO SPELOLOGICO”**  Published in 1994 for the 40th anniversary of the CNSAS (1954-1994);  Ø  **50 ANNI DI INFORTUNISTICA SPELEOLOGICA IN ITALIA (1947-1997)**  by Pino Guidi and Aurelio Pavanello;  Ø  **Infortunistica Speleologica in Italia: CINQUE ANNI DI INCIDENTI IN GROTTA E IN FORRA (1998-2002)**  by Pino Guidi and Aurelio Pavanello;  All the material listed can be borrowed for consultation from the club library; please refer to the library rules for loan procedures.
