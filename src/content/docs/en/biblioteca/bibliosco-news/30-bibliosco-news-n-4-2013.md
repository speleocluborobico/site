JOURNALS  The following publications have arrived at the clubhouse:  **SPELEOLOGIA EMILIANA** (Journal of the Federazione Speleologica Regionale dell’Emilia Romagna) no. 3 – year 2012    Contents: see attached pdf file  For lovers of artificial cavities, the two issues published in 2012 of the following are available:    **OPERA IPOGEA**(Six-monthly journal of the Commissione Nazionale Cavità Artificiali of the S.S.I.)  no. 1 (Jan-Jun 2012) – no. 2 (Jul-Dec 2012)  Index:  see attached pdf files    OL BÜS    As for our club journal, it has been decided that a complete set from no. 1 to no. 15 will be kept in the Library for consultation on the premises only; additional copies of issues no. 7 to no. 15 can instead be borrowed.  MULTIMEDIA    During the FSLo assembly held in Pogliana di Bisuschio on 2 March, the following DVDs were handed over to the club:  1.       **OSSERVATORIO DELLE AREE CARSICHE LOMBARDE** **(Dic 2011)**  This is the result of the various studies carried out on the regional Karst Areas for the Project entrusted to the FSLo by Regione Lombardia; for a detailed presentation visit the following link: <http://speleolombardia.wordpress.com/progetti/progetto-regionale-osservatorio-sulle-aree-carsiche/>  2.      **RADUNO SPELEOLOGICO REGIONALE** **LOMBARDO – Erba 6-7 Ottobre 2012**  It contains two DVDs with the various presentations and films shown at the last regional gathering;  here is the list  Disc 1 – PowerPoint presentations (71.6 MB):  1.       Linee guida per la gestione delle aree carsiche. Applicazione al caso dell’Alpe del Vicerè – Valle Bova  2.      Le nostre scoperte 1949-53 nel Buco della Niccolina e nel complesso Zelbio-Tacchi – by Rodolfo Pozzi (GSC)  3.      Le esplorazioni in Val Nosè attorno agli anni ‘70 – by Adriano Vanin (GGM)  4.      L’esplorazione della Stoppani – by Alberto Buzio (GGM)  5.      Storia del complesso del Nosè 2003-2011 – by Luana Aimar-Antonio Premazzi (SCE)    Disc 2 – films (1h 44’):  1.       Giunzione! – by Valeria Nava and Davide Corengia (InGrigna!)  2.      Ottavo Ingresso – by Pamela Romano (SCE)  3.      Nel cuore del Golem – by Luca Pedrali and Nadia Bocchi  4.      Sorgente del Tof - by Nadia Bocchi and Luca Pedrali  5.      Bueno Fonteno – immersione sifone Smeraldo – by Progetto Sebino  6.      10 anni di “InGrigna!” – by Antonio Premazzi and Virginia Mandracchia (InGrigna!)  7.      Terzo Mondo – secondo ingresso – by Speleo Club CAI Erba  The journals and multimedia material described above can be borrowed for consultation from the club library; please refer to the library regulations for loan procedures. |  |  |  |  |
