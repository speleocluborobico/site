A rich and substantial issue

​dedicated to what was collected during the “Speleopolis Underground” Gathering held in Casola Valsenio from 30/10 to 3/11/2013.

​Given the large number of publications acquired, for convenience I will split their description over several issues. In this first part I will tell you about the books:

​The following titles were received as gifts:

Ø  **IL TESORO DI SMOG** by Fabrizio Proietti of the Gruppo Grotte “Pipistrelli” CAI Terni, published in 2009, recounts the experience of the discovery (1962) and exploration (from 1982) of the Grotta di Cittareale (Rieti); it includes a technical data sheet on the cave;

Ø  **50 ANNI DI STORIA DEL GRUPPO GROTTE PIPISTRELLI CAI TERNI**, published by the group of the same name, also in 2009;

Ø  **ERMADA** by Dario Marini De Canedolo, of the Gruppo Speleologico Flondar, published in 2007, is about Monte Ermada and includes the relevant topographic map as an attachment;

Ø  **TRE GROTTE IN RICORDO DI CARLO MARCHESETTI**, published by the Gruppo Speleologico Flondar of Duino-Aurisina (Trieste) in 2013, is about the Grotta dell’Orso, the Grotta Azzurra di Samatorza and the Dolina Kavška, and includes the surveys of the three caves as attachments;

Ø  **PAESAGGI CARSICI NEL FRIULI VENEZIA GIULIA**, published by the Regione Autonoma Friuli Venezia Giulia;

Ø  **GROTTE DI GUERRA SUL CARSO** by Franco Gherlizza, published in 2011, covers underground itineraries in some First World War caves on the Carso Triestino and Goriziano;

Ø  **CANTADE DE LIKOFF** by Mario Gherbaz and Franco Gherlizza, published in 1995 for the 50th anniversary of the founding of the Club Alpinistico Triestino, contains a collection of “caving” songs, i.e. songs for caving parties/gatherings;

Ø  **LA GROTTA DELLE TORRI DI SLIVIA SUL CARSO TRIESTINO**, published in 1996 by the Federazione Speleologica Triestina, contains the survey of the cave;

Ø  **LA PREVENZIONE DEGLI INCIDENTI NEGLI IPOGEI NATURALI E ARTIFICIALI** by Franco Gherlizza, published in 2010 by the Scuola di Speleologia “Ennio Gherlizza” of the Club Alpinistico Triestino, also includes a very detailed PowerPoint presentation;

Ø  **GUIDA ALLA GROTTA NERA**, published in 2007, edited by Giovanni Boschian and Maurizio Anselmi, describes the palaeoethnological finds of the Grotta Nera near Basovizza (Trieste), which tell the story of the settlement of the Carso from its origins to 6000 years ago;

Ø  **LE GROTTE DEL CARSO: TESORI DA SVELARE**: a booklet published by the Regione Autonoma Friuli Venezia Giulia as a handbook on Caving – Schools – Tourism; it describes the tourist attractions of the Carso Triestino and the groups/associations to contact in order to visit them.

and also two publications dedicated to canyoning:

Ø  **VALUTAZIONE DEL RISCHIO DA PIENA IN FORRA**, edited by Paolo Madonia for the Associazione Italiana Canyoning in 2000: a technical and practical manual offering a first approach to assessing the hazard of a gorge with respect to flood events;

Ø  **GLI AMBIENTI DI FORRA** – Parte prima: elementi di geologia ed idrologia, by Paolo Madonia, published in 2006, again for the A.I.C..

Finally, the following was purchased:

Ø  **MARGUAREIS PER VIAGGIATORI** – Guida ai fenomeni carsici delle Alpi Liguri, published in 2000 by the Associazione Gruppi Speleologici Piemontesi.

​Happy reading and see you next time.
