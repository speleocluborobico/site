**GATHERING SPECIAL - Part 2**

Second issue dedicated to the material collected at the “Speleopolis Underground” Gathering held in Casola from 30/10 to 3/11/2013. 

This time we talk about:

JOURNALS

Several back issues of a number of journals were obtained, namely:

Ø  **SPELEOLOGIA** (Journal of the Società Speleologica Italiana) – issues 17 (1987), 22 (1990), 26-27 (1992), 33 (1995), 34 (1996); the collection is now almost complete, only no. 3 is missing, so if anyone manages to find it, please get in touch.

At this address, instead, you can preview some of the contents of the next issue, due out in December: <http://www.ssi.speleo.it/index.php?option=com_content&view=article&id=294&Itemid=238>

Ø  **TUTTOCAT** (Internal newsletter of the Club Alpinistico Triestino) – Years 2006, 2007, 2008, 2011.

At this address you can view the newsletters online:

<http://www.cat.ts.it/index.php?option=com_content&view=article&id=121&Itemid=130>

Ø  **LA NOSTRA SPELEOLOGIA** (Journal of the Gruppo Grotte Club Alpinistico Triestino) – single issue 2004.

Some issues of the journal can be viewed at this address:

<http://www.cat.ts.it/index.php?option=com_content&view=article&id=127&Itemid=129>

Ø  **PROGRESSIONE** (Bulletin of the Commissione Grotte Eugenio Boegan of the Società Alpina delle Giulie, Trieste section of the C.A.I.) – issues 44  and 45 (2001).

All issues of the journal can be viewed and downloaded in pdf format here:

<http://www.boegan.it/gli-archivi/progressione/>

Ø  **IN SCIÖ FÕNDO** (Bulletin of the Associazione Speleologica Genovese San Giorgio) – issue 5 (2003);

The articles published in the various issues of the journal can be viewed here:

<http://www.asgsangiorgio.it/pubblicazioni/pubblicazioni.html>

Ø  **TALP** (Journal of the Federazione Speleologica Toscana) – purchased the complete collection

of issues 1 to 45 (1989-2012), except for issues 9 and 19 (out of print), which were however obtained as high-resolution pdfs from the FST website.

At this address you can search all the articles published in issues 1 to 19 and download the corresponding pdfs: <http://www.speleotoscana.it/programmi_php/pubblicazioni/menu.php>

Ø  **CANYONING** (Newsletter of the Associazione Italiana Canyoning) – issues 0 (1998), 19 (2007), 23 (2009), 24- 25 (2010), 26-27 (2011), 28 (2012).

On this page you can read a good number of the journal's back issues online: <http://issuu.com/dallarik/stacks/ea8addce534e4da39d8838f4f0bad248>

These, on the other hand, are the journals published recently and received at Casola:

Ø  **NOTIZIARIO 2013 (**CAI- Sezione di Busto Arsizio) – received hot off the press from our friends of the Gruppo Grotte di Busto Arsizio; the section dedicated to the Gruppo Grotte is substantial and interesting.

Ø  **Q.4000** (CAI – Sezione di Erba) – in this case too, received just out from our friends of the Speleo Club Erba, who edited the rich caving section of the journal.

Online version at the following address: <http://cai.erba.org/nuovo/images/pdf/Q4000/Q4K_2013.pdf>

Ø  **STUDI E RICERCHE** (publication of the Societa' di Studi Carsici "A.F.Lindner" - Fogliano (GO)) – Single issue 2011 – Volume 7

NB: the publication can also be downloaded from the internet at the following link:

<http://www.studicarsici.it/studiricerche2012.pdf>

Ø  **TALP** (Journal of the Federazione Speleologica Toscana) – we have taken out a subscription to the journal for 2013 and 2014 (two issues per year) and received issue 46 (June 2013). This issue contains a very interesting article on the survey of the Complesso carsico del Monte Corchia.

At this link  <http://www.speleotoscana.it/talp_online/category/talp-online/>   you can view videos and in-depth material related to the articles in the latest issues.

Ø  **SPELEOLOGICAL  ABSTRACT** (Speleological Bibliographic Bulletin published by the Union Internationale de Spéléologie) – issues 47/48 (2008-2009) and 48/49 (2010-2011).

These two volumes were given to us when we renewed our SSI membership (as a group) and contain the list of the world's caving publications for the years concerned. The two volumes come with two CDs containing the digital version of the list.

Happy reading and see you next time.
